RHYTHM: a drum machine and sampler for the R36S
https://github.com/willbearfruits/r36s-rhythm

You need an R36S with ArkOS on its card.

1. Turn the R36S off and put its SD card in a computer.
2. Open the EASYROMS partition and the "ports" folder in it. (On an R36S with two
   cards, the games may be on the second card.)
3. Copy Rhythm.sh and the rhythm folder from this zip into "ports", so you have
   ports/Rhythm.sh and ports/rhythm/rhythm.
4. Put the card back and turn the R36S on: Ports > Rhythm.

START plays and stops. SELECT opens the menu. Hold L1 and the D-pad and A B X Y
play the drums. Hold SELECT + START for a second and a half to leave.

Your sounds: put WAV files in ports/rhythm/card/SAMPLES (folders inside are fine;
the folder appears after the first start, or make it yourself). Projects are in
card/PROJECTS, renders in card/RENDERS, sampled takes in card/RECORDINGS.

A newer version copies over this one the same way; your card folder stays.

If something goes wrong, rhythm/log.txt and rhythm/stdout.txt say what happened.
